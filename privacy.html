Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who this applies to

Zane is a personal automation tool operated by and for a single individual. It has no other users and collects no data from anyone else.

Google account access

Zane requests the drive.file scope, which permits access only to files it creates itself. It cannot read or modify any other file in the user's Google Drive.

Data handling

Documents created by Zane are stored in the operator's own Google Drive. Content sent to Zane may be processed by third-party language model providers in order to generate responses. No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or transferred to any other party.

Retention and deletion

The operator may revoke Zane's access at any time via Google Account settings, and may delete any file it has created at any time.
